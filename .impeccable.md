# Design Context — AIVIVE

## Users

Image-makers who want a quiet, curated tool — photographers, illustrators, writers, designers. They come to browse beautiful work first; the act of generating is secondary, almost a side effect of inspiration. Context of use is mostly evenings on a laptop, lingering on a page like flipping through a magazine.

## Brand Personality

Three words: **curated, generous, contemplative.**

Voice: An editor's voice, not a salesperson's. Confident, plain, slightly literary. Numbers and editions, not features and benefits. Never says "powerful AI" — always says what someone made.

## Aesthetic Direction

- **Tone**: Editorial / gallery. Museum exhibition caption energy, not SaaS product page.
- **References**: Independent print magazines (e.g., Apartamento, The Gentlewoman), Are.na, museum-shop catalogue, vinyl liner notes.
- **Anti-references**: Generic SaaS hero with feature cards, blog-with-icons bento grids, gradient hero text, glowing accents on dark, snowflake/particle backgrounds, glassmorphism for decoration.
- **Theme**: Dark warm — charcoal tinted toward amber (hue ~60). Bone-on-charcoal palette. Amber accent used sparingly for italic emphasis only.

## Type & Color (lock these in)

- **Display**: Bricolage Grotesque (variable). Used at large sizes for editorial headings, italics for accents.
- **Body**: Geist Sans. Restrained.
- **Mono**: Geist Mono. Reserved for section markers (§ 001), edition numbers (ED. 014), small caps tracking ≥ 0.22em.
- **Avoid**: Inter, DM Sans, Outfit, Plus Jakarta, Instrument family, Playfair, Cormorant, Crimson, Fraunces, Newsreader, Space Mono — all banned for AIVIVE.
- **Palette** (oklch): bg `0.13 0.006 60` · surface `0.19 0.01 60` · border `0.27 0.012 60` · fg `0.96 0.012 80` · muted `0.72 0.013 65` · accent `0.82 0.135 65`.
- **Wrapper**: `[data-theme="editorial"]` scoping in `theme.css` so landing/gallery can use this palette without breaking the rest of the app (which keeps the aqua/ink theme).

## Design Principles

1. **Images are the protagonist.** UI gets out of the way. No card chrome, no buttons in front of art, no captions over images unless on hover.
2. **Sections are chapters, not modules.** Numbered (§ 001, § 002) like editorial sections. Each section breathes.
3. **Italic is the only accent.** No colored highlights, no gradients. Color emphasis comes through *italic + amber*, used once or twice per section max.
4. **Mono is metadata, never body.** Reserved for edition numbers, contributor names, taglines. Body text is always Geist Sans.
5. **Motion is restrained.** Scroll-driven fade-in (8% threshold), hover scale ≤ 1.04, 700–1200ms ease-out. No bounce, no elastic, no parallax, no auto-play.

## Layout System

- **Grid**: 12-col, 24px gutter, max-width 1480px, page padding 20px (mobile) / 40px (md).
- **Masonry**: CSS `columns` (1 / 2 / 3 / 4 at sm/lg/xl). Items use `break-inside: avoid` and `mb-4 md:mb-6`.
- **Asymmetry preferred** — headlines indent on selected lines, content blocks span 7-11 columns rather than centered 8.
